📖 RESOURCE THUMBNAILS
──────────────────────
Place thumbnail images for Notes & Resources page cards.

Accepted formats: .jpg, .png, .webp
Suggested size: 600×400px (card thumbnail ratio).

Examples:

  - biochemistry-notes.jpg  → biochemistry resource thumbnail
  - pharmacology-summary.jpg → pharmacology quick-reference card
  - clinical-skills.jpg     → clinical skills cheat sheet
